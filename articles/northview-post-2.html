🏚 Northview Gardens — Post 2

An Update on Northview Gardens Apartments

Since the first post, additional documented incidents have come to light.

The timeline now looks like this.

July 2024 — water pressure loss. Mold. Non-working toilets. Management took pictures and left. Residents could not get answers.

January 2025 — pipe failure. Residents without water for days. Temporary hotel housing arranged.

May 2025 — structure fire. Martinsville Fire and EMS responded.

October 2025 — another fire. This time both Martinsville Fire Department and Henry County Public Safety responded simultaneously.

That last detail matters.

Two separate emergency response agencies showing up to the same property is not a coincidence. It is what happens when a city and county boundary line runs directly through a housing complex. Each jurisdiction sends its own response. Neither has sole accountability.

Four documented incidents in fifteen months. A federal inspection score from 2019. An out-of-state management company that has not responded to press inquiry.

The response deadline is still May 21, 2026.
