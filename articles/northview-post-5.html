🏚 Northview Gardens — Post 5

What the Record Shows: Who Owns Northview Gardens?

Henry County's own parcel records identify the current owner of Northview Gardens Apartments at 755 Stultz Road, Martinsville, as Rigamaz Northview LLC, with a listed address of 211 N Union Street, Suite 122, Alexandria, Virginia.

A search of the Virginia State Corporation Commission Clerk's Information System returns no records for Rigamaz Northview LLC. A search for "Rigamaz" alone returns no records.

The same search was conducted in Delaware, Maryland, and North Carolina. The North Carolina Secretary of State's office confirmed zero records as of June 2, 2026. North Carolina's business name availability tool shows the name as available — meaning it has never been registered there.

Rigamaz Northview LLC does not appear to exist as a legally registered entity in any jurisdiction searched.

The address listed in county records — 211 N Union Street — is a commercial registered agent office in Alexandria hosting approximately 39 entities in Virginia SCC records. Rigamaz Northview LLC is not among them.

The previous owner of record, per county GIS, is Northview Limited Partnership, percentage Vista. Virginia SCC records show Northview Limited Partnership, Entity ID L0152019, was formed in 1998 with a stated duration expiring January 1, 2025. As of this publication, the SCC continues to list that entity as Active. Its principal office is recorded as Suite 800 at 755 Stultz Road — the property address itself, where no Suite 800 exists.

Five documented inquiries have been sent to Vista Capital and Chris Lawless. None have received a response. Reporting continues.

The property was built in 1976, is zoned SR, and carries a total assessed value of $2,932,600 per Henry County records. It is currently occupied by residents.
