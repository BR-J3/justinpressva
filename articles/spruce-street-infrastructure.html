Nobody's Pole: A Hazard on Spruce Street and the City That Won't Claim It

A utility pole at or near 1204 Spruce Street in Martinsville, Virginia has been visibly leaning and deteriorating for nearly two decades. Google Street View imagery documents the pole's condition as far back as February 2008. By May 2025, the lean had worsened considerably, with overhead lines under visible tension.

The pole sits along a heavily traveled residential corridor at the intersection of Spruce Street, Brookdale Street, and Windsor Lane — directly across from Spruce Village Apartments and within proximity of Martinsville Health and Rehab at 1607 Spruce Street Extension.

A resident of that address has reportedly made repeated contacts with both the City of Martinsville and Brightspeed Communications over approximately two years seeking resolution. The city has reportedly directed the resident to Brightspeed as the responsible party. Brightspeed has reportedly denied ownership. Neither party has taken corrective action.

The City of Martinsville's Public Works Department lists Street Cleaning and Right-of-Way Maintenance among its stated responsibilities. The location falls entirely within city limits.

Brightspeed — formed in 2022 when Apollo Global Management acquired consumer telephone and internet operations from Lumen Technologies across 20 states — completed a fiber network buildout in Martinsville as recently as November 2025 and has received federal BEAD program funding for infrastructure deployment in Henry County and Martinsville.
