🏚 Northview Gardens — Full Series

Every Player. Every Documented Fact.

Northview Gardens Apartments sits at 755 Stultz Road in Martinsville, Virginia. It is a 100-unit affordable housing complex operating under a federal Housing Assistance Payment contract. The following is the complete documented record as reported by Justin Press VA from May through June 2026.

Key Facts on Record

📍 755 Stultz Road, Martinsville, Virginia — boundary line between City of Martinsville and Henry County runs directly through the complex

🏢 Managed by Vista Capital Management Group, Greenville, South Carolina — has not responded to press inquiry

📋 Last federal inspection score: 83b — November 2019

💰 HUD Housing Assistance Payment contract covering 20% of units through September 2034

⚖️ Five civil cases across two courts spanning eight years

🔥 Ten documented incidents spanning a decade — violence, infrastructure failures, fires

🏛️ Current county GIS owner of record: Rigamaz Northview LLC — not registered in Virginia, Delaware, Maryland, or North Carolina

📁 Previous ownership entity: Northview Limited Partnership — duration expired January 1, 2025, still listed Active by SCC, principal office is a fictional Suite 800 at the property address
