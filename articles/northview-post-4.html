🏚 Northview Gardens — Post 4

755 Stultz Road Has More to Say

The documented incident history at this address — spanning violence, infrastructure failures, and fire responses dating to 2015 — was established in previous reporting. What follows is what has come to light since.

Justin Press VA contacted Vista Capital Management Group on May 11, 2026 with three specific questions about inspection history, repair documentation, and current infrastructure conditions. The response deadline was May 21, 2026. No response was received.

A community member contacted the property manager separately in May 2026. No response.

Justin Press VA contacted Collinsville District Supervisor Chris Lawless on May 19, 2026 in two capacities — at his county office and at his private business. In November 2025, while still supervisor-elect, Lawless visited the property and observed standing water in the parking lot. Star News TV reported on November 9, 2025 that Lawless said he would look into the possible leaks and follow up with more information as it becomes available. No follow-up has been publicly documented. A response deadline of May 30, 2026 has been set.

In July 2024, resident John Mahan spoke on record to the Henry County Enterprise and Star News TV about conditions at the property. He described no running commodes, discolored water unfit to drink, mold, and ongoing plumbing failures. He also stated that repair companies had refused to return to the property due to unresolved payment disputes — and that at least one resident who complained about a leak was threatened with eviction.

When media arrived to ask questions in July 2024, management did not speak with reporters.

The Virginia court record is also part of the public record.

A search of Henry County Circuit Court and Martinsville Circuit Court records surfaces the following:

2017 — a resident filed a General Tort Liability claim against Northview Limited Partnership, National Housing and Education Trust, and Vista Capital Management Group.

2018 — a second resident filed a complaint against the same three entities. Final order May 2023.

2022 — Northview Limited Partnership filed an unlawful detainer action against residents. Judgment for the plaintiff.

2023 — a third resident filed a General Tort Liability claim against Northview Limited Partnership and Vista Capital Management Group. Final order October 2024.

2025 — a contract action was filed against Northview Limited Partnership by an outside entity. Dismissed April 2026. A counterclaim filed by Northview in September 2025 remains pending.

That is five civil cases across two courts spanning eight years at one address.

The ownership entity — Northview Limited Partnership — has been registered with the Virginia State Corporation Commission since September 14, 1998. Its principal office of record is listed as 755 Stultz Road Suite 800, Martinsville, Virginia — the property address itself, where no Suite 800 exists. Its registered agent is a commercial forwarding service in Glen Allen, Virginia.

The last publicly available federal inspection score for this property remains 83b, dated November 2019. The property continues to operate under a HUD Housing Assistance Payment contract covering 20 percent of its units through September 2034.

Four infrastructure incidents. Five civil cases. Five unanswered inquiries. A federal inspection record nearly seven years old. A resident on record describing contractor payment disputes and eviction threats against those who complained.

People still live there.
