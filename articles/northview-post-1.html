🏚 Northview Gardens — Post 1

Two Governments. One Property.

Northview Gardens Apartments sits at 755 Stultz Road in Martinsville, Virginia.

It is a 100-unit affordable housing complex built in 1976. It operates under a federal Housing Assistance Payment contract covering 20% of its units through September 2034. Federal dollars flow through this property every month.

In July 2024 residents lost water pressure. Management took pictures and left. Residents could not get answers.

In January 2025 a pipe failure left residents without water for several days. The city arranged temporary hotel housing while the situation was evaluated.

In May 2025 a structure fire broke out at the property. Martinsville Fire and EMS responded. No injuries were reported.

Three documented incidents in less than one year. No public resolution on record for any of them.

The property is managed by Vista Capital Management Group — headquartered in Greenville, South Carolina. A community member contacted the property manager in May 2026. No response. Justin Press VA contacted Vista Capital on May 11, 2026 with questions about current conditions and inspection history. No response yet.

The last publicly available federal inspection score for this property is 83b — dated November 2019. That is nearly seven years ago.

Here is why accountability is complicated at this address.

The city of Martinsville and Henry County share a boundary line that runs directly through this property. Depending on which unit you live in, you fall under a different government's jurisdiction.

Two governments. One property. One out-of-state management company. Residents in the middle.

Justin Press VA has a response deadline of May 21, 2026.
